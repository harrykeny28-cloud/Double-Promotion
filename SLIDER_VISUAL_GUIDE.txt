NAVIGATION FLOW:
================================================================================

User clicks category card (e.g., "T-Shirts"):
        ↓
Category card is clickable link
        ↓
Navigates to /collection/tshirts
        ↓
Collection page loads
        ↓
Products filtered to show T-Shirts only
        ↓
User sees T-Shirt products in grid

User clicks "View All →":
        ↓
Navigates to /collection
        ↓
All categories shown at top
        ↓
All products shown in grid below
